Dates

The papers give the vegetable management only as seasons (Brennan and Boyd 2012: “romaine lettuce from May to June or July”; White et al. 2020b: “from May to June”). Brennan and Boyd (2012) Table 2 gives each winter’s residue incorporation, cover crop planting, last cover crop irrigation and termination (flail mowing) dates for the six annually cover-cropped systems, and Brennan and Smith (2017) give the Year 4 and Year 8 planting dates for socs_sys1 and socs_sys2. Those rows carry the printed dates (min_date = max_date, or the printed range); every other row carries a window built from the papers’ wording (table below), with the source summarized in notes. Point dates are never invented.

Three things the rows state as assumptions or caveats:

  • The spring spading after an annual cover crop is taken as the Table 2 termination day: Brennan and Boyd (2012) say the cover crops “were terminated by flail mowing and the residue was incorporated with one pass using a spader”, but do not date the spading.
  • socs_sys1 and socs_sys2 take the annual systems’ Table 2 residue incorporation dates and bed preparation windows, because Data in Brief says tillage and harvest schedules were the same in every system.
  • Table 2 prints the Year 2 residue incorporation as approximate (≈ 11 September 2004); those rows keep 11 September and say so. Brennan and Smith (2017) put the 2010 incorporation 12 days before 25 October (13 October) where Table 2 says 14 October, so those rows carry 13 to 14 October.

The calendar runs from the 30 September 2003 residue incorporation before the Year 1 cover crop to the Year 8 spading in August 2011. Year 1’s second crop is baby leaf spinach; Years 2–7 grow broccoli; Year 8 grows lettuce only. The windows:

In 2009 the lettuce, compost and pelleted fertilizer windows are only 24 to 31 May, because White et al. (2020b) report a 72 day decomposition after wet spring weather and Brennan and Boyd (2012) plant the lettuce in May.

SOC, total N and nitrate-N rows use the fall sampling window of each year (August for Year 8). The Year 0 and Year 6 rows instead carry 13 October 2003 and 30 October 2009, which White et al. (2020b) give as the dates of additional samples frozen for microbial biomass; the paper does not say the 0–30 cm composite samples were taken on those days. The bulk density rows have their own date question. Both are in Open follow-ups.
